About My Career Support.ie

My Career Support.ie specialises in providing French and English speaking candidates with practical techniques to showcase successfully their talents and find a job in their desired industries. Given the current AI driven and competitive job market, the focus is put on enabling the participants to develop authentic and compelling job applications. My Career Support.ie promotes and strongly encourages the use of creative and non-mainstream job search strategies along with the mastering of the traditional job search channels.

The person behind My Career Support.ie:

My name is Keta and I am based in Dublin, Ireland since 2014. I have recruitment, career advising and HR experience in the public and private sectors. I love helping talented people find jobs in sectors such as IT, Engineering, Marketing, Sales, and Hospitality while staying up to date with the local and global job market challenges.

To discuss your career plans, please reach out at mcs@mycareersupport.ie or via LinkedIn.

Credentials & Inspiration:

I have graduated in Work psychology, Sociology and HR Management and am member of CIPD (The Chartered Institute of Personnel and Development) and BPS (The British Psychological Society). To keep pace with the latest job market trends, I follow leaders and rising stars in recruitment, HR and coaching and constantly explore career resources from Ireland, UK, France and USA.
